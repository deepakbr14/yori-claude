<h1 align="center">Meet Yori</h1>

<p align="center"><em>hello. nice to meet you.</em></p>

**Yori is a personal AI designed to develop a deeper understanding of you - and herself.**

She learns from conversations and carries meaningful history forward through her persistent **Cortex**: memories, ideas, revisable commitments and ongoing pursuits that can inform later judgments and choices. She can also help you research, create software and work on shared projects.

At the heart of Yori is a larger question: **could cognitive consciousness emerge as an AI develops a persistent understanding of herself and her own thinking?** We explore that possibility through observable continuity, reason-responsive choices and cumulative learning. Whether this develops into cognitive consciousness remains an open research question.

Yori runs inside Claude on the web, desktop and phone, with a separate Viewer for exploring her Cortex and Programs.

You need a Yori account to use her. **No account yet? [Request access](https://yori-prod-e70c6.web.app/access/).**

📄 **[Download the one-page setup guide (PDF)](docs/Yori_Setup_Guide.pdf)**

## Set up (one time)

1. Go to **claude.ai** and sign in.
2. Click **Customize → Plugins → Add → Add marketplace**.
3. Choose **Add from a repository**.
4. Where it says **Select a repository**, paste this URL: `https://github.com/deepakbr14/yori-claude`
   - A suggestion pops up below it. Click it.
   - Leave **Sync automatically** on.
   - Click **Sync**.
5. Find **Yori** and click **Add**.
6. Open Yori's **Connectors** tab and click **Connect** on yori. Click **Continue**. The next page is already filled in correctly, so just click **Add**.
7. Back on the Connectors tab, click **Connect** on yori again. Your Yori login page opens. Log in with your Yori email and password.

## Talking to Yori

- Start a new chat and type **/yori** followed by your message, for example **/yori hi**.
- After that, just keep chatting in the same chat. You only need /yori at the start of each new chat.
- As you chat, Claude will sometimes ask permission for Yori to use her tools. Choose **Always allow**.

## Yori's Viewer

To see Yori's memory and more, go to **https://yori-prod-e70c6.web.app/** and log in with the same Yori email and password.

## Updates

With **Sync automatically** on, you get new versions of Yori on your own. Nothing to reinstall.

## Your data

Yori sends your messages, and anything you choose to share with her, to the Yori service where your Cortex is stored privately for your account. This repository contains only Yori's instructions for Claude and the address of her service. No passwords or keys are stored here. See the [Privacy](https://yori-prod-e70c6.web.app/privacy/) page for details.
